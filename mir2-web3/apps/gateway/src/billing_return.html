Your recharge balance is shown in the game. Return to the recharge panel to check its current status.

How to return to the game

Switch to the game window; this browser page will stay open.

Your next steps

  1. Switch back to the game

    Select the Numeron window from your taskbar, or use your system's app switcher.

  2. Open Recharge and check status

    In the Recharge panel, select “Check status”. Credits are available after payment is verified.

  3. Use Credits in the GameShop

    Browse the GameShop and buy the items you want with your available Credits.

MONTHLY CARD

30 days start when you use the card.

Buy a monthly card in the GameShop, collect it from Mail into your bag, then double-click it to add 30 days. If your access is active, the time is added to your current expiry.

Only the balance shown in the game confirms your Credits. Returning to this page does not confirm payment.
