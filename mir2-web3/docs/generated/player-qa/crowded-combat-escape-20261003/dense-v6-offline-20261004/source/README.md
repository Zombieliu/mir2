## Qualification and case verdicts

The release embeds its monster catalogue. This prepared personal store cannot pin or seed monster spawns. The driver uses normal original D021 CaveBat spawning and ordinary movement/lure. No debug move, `stage5`, `qa`, kill, spawn, teleport or event API is available through the driver. All accepted CaveBat IDs must be live hostile AI 0, currently adjacent, with two actual aimed attack cycles at least 2,400ms apart in a 6,200ms window. Require at least 14 positive normal owner DamageIndicator receipts, exact owner HP decline and a fresh empty static-walkable three-cell cardinal exit without transfers, doors, safe zones, hazards or genuine paralysis/freeze. Two attackers repeated many times, neutral actors, all Miss/zero damage, unknown vitals and rounded percentage zero cannot qualify.

DamageIndicator has the victim ID, not each attacker. Seven independent attack identities are qualified through real actor positions/facing and source family; the receipt does not fabricate per-attacker positive damage attribution. Exact HP/MP provenance is annotated only from real absolute `worldSnapshot`, `UserInformation`, `HealthChanged`, or owner `Death` lifecycle receipts. Percentage/unknown observations remain non-exact. All classifiers require a declared finite closed interval and the actual owner ID, so a later unrelated death/cast or another character's receipts cannot relabel a past trial. HP increases from actual ordinary medicine/regen are retained in the HP ledger; incoming damage is never dropped to improve results.

Each class gets three ordinary attack/escape and held-input/stop trials. Wizard/Taoist additionally get three cast/escape and premature-recast trials when their normally purchased spells are eligible. Attack and cast source movement barriers are 550ms and 600ms; normal global spell time is 1,800ms. The diagnostic escape ceiling is 1,500ms. Require incoming positive owner damage during the pending escape, preserve committed action/effect receipts, and retain all late ACKs/corrections. A move with no incoming positive owner hit while pending is inconclusive for the continuous-hit deadline.

A committed cast requires the actual matching ordinary Magic input sequence, `MagicCast.spell` matching that input, and owner `ObjectMagic` with the same spell and `cast === true`; absent/false cast is preparation. The first matching pair and an actual absolute-MP receipt showing spend from an explicit absolute baseline must all occur inside the declared closed commitment window. A cached MP value on an unrelated animation is insufficient. For cast/escape this window closes before the Walk input; later projectile/Healing/effect receipts are retained in a separately bounded effect window, and cannot retroactively supply missing commitment proof. Premature recast additionally proves the matching second Magic input and first-window pairing/MP spend before the second input. Actual owner Death or exact HP 0 during the second waiting window is failed regardless of cooldown/pair/coverage prerequisites, never passed as cooldown rejection. Wrong spells, unknown/percentage zeros and future/foreign receipts stay separate or inconclusive.

After the final accepted step, collect at least five seconds of independent snapshots and continuous receipts. No extra movement/cast/combat input is sent in this quiet window. The held series uses ordinary Walk intents every 90ms for 850ms; after stopping it permits at most one already pending step within 1,500ms, followed by five stable seconds. Extra queued steps, a late correction, owner death or drift stays failed. Network intents do not prove the native right-button latch, sprite hit-testing or visual animation.

Eight-way occupancy is a separate optional case: verify all eight live monster cells and rejection of a normal walk, kill the selected blocker through actual normal attacks, require its ObjectDied receipt and the remaining seven qualified attackers, then verify the opened exit ACK and stop. Unformable eight-way geometry is inconclusive; a seven ring is not a substitute.

The optional death case runs in a new private clone of the sealed checkpoint. It uses unchanged hostile damage to kill the canonical owner, requires positive incoming damage plus owner Death and exact HP 0, then probes valid ordinary movement/attack/eligible cast without successful living actions. If there is no live adjacent target, attack denial cannot be claimed merely from an invalid distant target. A caster barrier is claimed only from a matching actual Magic input whose prior in-window owner snapshot independently proves a normally learned spell, known source MP cost, sufficient absolute MP, clear cast control, and otherwise-valid target: live adjacent hostile for FireBall, self for Healing (ignoring only owner death, the barrier under test). Input target coordinates must match that observed target. Missing target/MP/book/input records `castBarrier.status=not-applicable`, `verified=false`; the caster's combined death-action result remains inconclusive. Warrior cast is explicitly not applicable. A preparatory `ObjectMagic(cast=false)` is retained separately and never counted as a committed cast. Normal logout/relogin must preserve the actual terminal death, townRevive must restore positive HP at the saved bind point, and five stable seconds plus another logout/relogin must prove that old input did not replay. An unexpected death in an earlier escape trial remains that trial's failure.

Natural formation failures remain inconclusive, and actual early deaths remain failed. `sevenMonsterMechanicsAccepted` can only reflect all applicable qualified cases; `fullP1Accepted`, native input/visual, human acceptance, ordinary progression and capacity acceptance always remain false. `RECEIPT-SCHEMA.json` expresses these public proof boundaries.
